For Roku and Samsung TVs  ·  Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version: Kendibah works with Roku and Samsung TVs on your Wi-Fi network. Most remote features work on both. A few depend on what the TV supports, and some need a setting turned on in the TV first.

  • On a Roku streaming player, as opposed to a Roku TV, Kendibah doesn't offer channels or inputs, and volume and power work only when the player can control the TV.
  • On Samsung TVs, what you type or dictate goes to Samsung's own on-screen keyboard. Apps that draw their own keyboard, such as YouTube and the Apple TV app, don't receive it.
  • Now Playing needs the TV to report what's playing. Only Roku does, so Now Playing works with Roku only. The remote's Live Activity on the Lock Screen, with Back, Home, Play/Pause, Mute and Power, works with every TV.
  • With a Roku, your iPhone's volume buttons control the TV while Kendibah is open on screen.

2. TV Settings You May Need to Turn On

Some features need a setting turned on in the TV. Menu names and paths may differ by model and software version. If yours look different, look for a setting with the same or a similar name.

Roku

Control by mobile apps

Settings > System > Advanced system settings > Control by mobile apps

Choose the option that lets mobile apps control the TV. The names of the choices depend on your Roku's software version, and an option that limits or turns off control will block Kendibah. Without this setting, the Roku refuses the remote's keys and the app list. Kendibah tells you when a Roku is blocking it.

Fast TV Start

Settings > System > Power

Helps the TV answer Kendibah while it's off, so Power can turn it on.

Samsung

Power On with Mobile

On most models: Settings > All Settings > Connection > Network > Expert Settings

On 2021 and earlier models: Settings > General > Network > Expert Settings

Lets your iPhone turn the TV on.

IP Remote

In the same Expert Settings menu as Power On with Mobile.

Some TVs need this turned on before they accept a remote over the network.

If you chose Deny by mistake

On most models: Settings > General > External Device Manager > Device Connection Manager > Device List

Remove Kendibah from the list, then pair the TV again in Kendibah and choose Allow.

Settings You May Need

Menus vary by model year, and none of these have been confirmed on a TV yet.

  • LG: turn on "Turn on via Wi-Fi" so Kendibah can turn the TV on. On 2021 to 2024 models it's under Settings > General > Devices > External Devices > TV On With Mobile, and on 2025 and later models under Settings > Support > IP control settings > Wake on LAN. On 2021 and earlier models, also turn on Quick Start+, which helps the TV answer Kendibah while it's off.
  • Google TV: turn on the TV's network standby setting so Kendibah can turn the TV on. It's called Remote start on Sony, Network Standby on TCL and Wake on LAN on Hisense.
  • Fire TV: keep HDMI-CEC turned on in the television's settings, so volume and power reach it. Your iPhone and the Fire TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network, with no VPN running on the Fire TV.
  • Vizio: set Power Mode to Quick Start instead of Eco Mode (Menu > System > Power Mode) so Kendibah can turn the TV on. Kendibah can't check this setting for you, so if it can't turn the TV on, look here first.
