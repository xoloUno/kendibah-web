The remote

Everything your remote does, on the phone in your hand.

Swipe on a trackpad to move around and tap to select, with Back, Home, Play/Pause, Mute and Power always in reach.

Your apps and inputs, one tap away.

Your TV's apps and inputs sit in one panel, in the order you choose. Give your inputs your own names, like "PlayStation" instead of "HDMI 2".

A whole remote on your Home Screen.

Add the Kendibah widget and control your TV without opening the app.

Build a remote in Control Center.

Add buttons to open the remote, play or pause, mute and power. After you use the remote, its buttons stay on the Lock Screen for a few minutes.

iPhone Duo

Made for iPhone and iPhone Duo.

Open it, and your TV's apps and inputs sit on one side of the fold, with the remote on the other. Set it down like a laptop, and the remote rests on the lower half.

And more

The little things, done right.

Type and dictate

Search on your TV with your iPhone's keyboard, or just say it.

Turn your TV on

Wake your TV from Kendibah. Some TVs need a setting turned on first.

Shortcuts and Siri

Mute the TV, open an app on a TV you name, or show its art.

Art Mode on The Frame

Switch a Samsung Frame to its art from your iPhone.

Now Playing on Roku

See what's playing on the Lock Screen, and use your iPhone's volume buttons while Kendibah is open.
